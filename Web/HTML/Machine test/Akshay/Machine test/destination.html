Paris, France
Paris, known as the City of Light, is famed for its art, fashion, and landmarks like the Eiffel Tower and the Louvre Museum.

London, England
London is the capital and largest city of both England and the United Kingdom, with a population of 8,945,309 in 2023.

Dubai
Dubai is the most populous city in the United Arab Emirates and the capital of the Emirate of Dubai.

Rome, Italy
Rome offers a mix of ancient ruins, Renaissance art, and delicious Italian cuisine. Visit the Colosseum and Vatican City.
